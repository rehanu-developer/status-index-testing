# Status Index

> Status Index helps recognized individuals and organizations in the United States build, claim, correct, and optimize Google Knowledge Panels, strengthening their presence in Google's Knowledge Graph for stronger branded search, zero-click visibility, and credibility.

Status Index builds "entity architecture" — connecting personal identity, organizational identity, and external authority sources (press, profiles, structured databases) — to help Google recognize and display a verified Knowledge Panel for founders, CEOs, entrepreneurs, authors, realtors, artists, and brands. Co-founded by Basit and Muneeb.

## Core pages

- [Home](https://statusindex.com/): Overview of Knowledge Panel services for individuals and companies. Explains what a Knowledge Panel is, why it matters for trust/visibility/E-E-A-T, and the core offering (entity audit & strategy, build & optimization, ongoing panel management).
- [Services](https://statusindex.com/services): Details the entity-based Knowledge Panel framework (personal identity, organizational identity, external authority sources) and the 5-step process: audit digital footprint, build structured schema markup, align presence across platforms, reinforce entity signals, strengthen Knowledge Graph connectivity. Lists client categories: Founders & CEOs, Entrepreneurs, Authors & Writers, Realtors, Artists, Companies & Brands.
