What is a Knowledge Panel?

A Knowledge Panel signals to users, journalists, and prospects that Google recognizes you as a credible entity. It strengthens your brand SERP, improves zero-click visibility, and increases perceived authority before a visitor ever reaches your site. In a search environment increasingly shaped by entity recognition and E-E-A-T, your panel is one of the clearest trust indicators available.
